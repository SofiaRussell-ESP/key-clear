Capstone Pitch

KEY_CLEAR

Reads solicitor correspondence. Translates it to plain English. Flags what needs a response. Drafts one for human approval.

Sofia Russell — Product Manager
The Problem

First-time buyers drown in dense legal correspondence — dozens of documents, no clear sense of what needs a response, or by when.

Why an agent, not a static translator

It has to classify, extract deadlines, flag risk, and draft — in one pass, with judgment about when to refuse.
